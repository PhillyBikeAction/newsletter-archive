July 19: Dr. Barbara Friedes 2-Year Memorial

It's been 2 years since we lost Dr. Barbara Friedes to a reckless driver on Spruce Street when she was hit from behind by a driver speeding in the unprotected bike lane.

Her death had a profound impact across the city and galvanized a movement for safer streets and concrete protected bike lanes for Spruce and Pine - the promise of which we are still waiting on two years later.

Join us for a community memorial event to reflect on her life, legacy, and the continued need for protected bike lanes.

Sunday July 19 | 8:30am - 9:30am
Meet at Barbara's Angel Bike 18th & Spruce

Bring a full reusable water bottle to pour into our symbolic memorial.

Add to calendar
 

July 24: West Market St Petition Press Rally

PBA begun 2026 by launching the West Market Street Petition in response to two pedestrian fatalities on the street last November. Since then, PBA has knocked on 1,000 doors and spent 30 volunteer hours canvasing to collect over 1400 signatures and gained the endorsements of Councilmember Jamie Gauthier, University City District, and numerous other community organizations.

Now, PBA will be hosting a press rally with State Rep. Rick Krajewski and other community organizations urging PennDOT implement our petition demands on July 24 at 11 am. Come show support for safer streets and bring signs for the press!
