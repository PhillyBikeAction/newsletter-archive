November 2026 Newsletter

Sign the South Street Bridge petition!

Sign the petition

Nearly 300 of you sent emails to the City demanding accelerated improvements to the South St Bridge bike lane after a woman was hit. You got their attention.

Last week, crews were out re-painting the bike lane and adding back long-missing flex posts. But, at least 10 have popped off within days of install. This bike lane needs real, permanent solutions. No more flex posts. We need concrete jersey barriers like they had during a construction detour in 2016 and a bike signal to remove the mixing zone. 

 

Another deadly month

In early October, police failed to respond to an appalling drag race hit-and-run incident that injured a cyclist on North Broad Street.

We also learned that an 82 year old man who was biking on the Spring Garden bridge in late September has died from his injuries.

On Sept 30, a 26 year old teacher Zoe Schemm was killed while walking across 19th and Lombard in Center City.

How to Volunteer with PBA

Many of you asked in the Community Survey for us to make volunteer opportunities easier to find.

We created a new Volunteer Opportunity mailing list, which you can opt into from your PBA Profile. We'll start using it once enough people enroll.

We also updated our Get Involved page to highlight the teams you can join.

Remember PBA is a truly grassroots organization: nothing happens without a dedicated person pushing it forward. If you see an opportunity for change in your neighborhood, fill out a project application to let us know how you want to use the resources PBA offers to advance our mission of safe biking in Philadelphia.
