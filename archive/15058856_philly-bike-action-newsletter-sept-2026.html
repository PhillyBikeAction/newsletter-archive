PBA Board Elections & Membership

Philly Bike Action has a democratically elected, all-volunteer Board of Directors that focuses on strategic planning and organizational governance. 

PBA Members can vote in the election or run for a Board seat. Our next election is November 2026.

Learn more about how to become a member. Learn more about our Board.

There will be an info session about board service after our Citywide Meeting on October 5 around 7:30pm at the Philadelphia Ethical Society.

News

  • Join Families for Safe Streets on Sept. 27 as they walk 9.4 miles along Route 13 to bring positive attention to automated speed cameras. Family and friends of traffic victims will share their stories and uplift how speed cameras could have prevented the loss of their loved ones.
  • 📝 Mention safer bike lanes in Center City District satisfaction survey!
  • 📝 Fill out the new Market East survey and tell the City to make protected bike lanes a priority. There's a specific question about protected bike lanes!

  • A concrete protected bike lane coming to Market St Bridge! Watch the video rendering of the design.
  • On Sept 11, join a bike ride with fellow bike advocates from the Boston Cyclists Union, who will be coming off a 5-day ride from Boston to Philly!
  • Falls Bridge Commuter Cafe: On Wednesday, Sept. 16, visit us on the Schuylkill River Trail between 7-9 am for free coffee and to chat about bike advocacy. Or, join a group commute to Center City with Philly Bike Train, meeting at 7:30 and departing at 7:45. Rain date is Sept. 30.

Apply for a Community Action Fund!

Have an idea to make biking better in your neighborhood? Philly Bike Action is now accepting applications for our new Community Action Fund, which provides grants of $250–$2,000 for community-led projects that create physical improvements for people biking in Philadelphia. Applications are due Friday, October 2, 2026.

We will be holding open office hours for potential applicants to ask questions on Friday, September 4 from noon to 1 pm on Zoom. Please register here.

See our website for more information.
