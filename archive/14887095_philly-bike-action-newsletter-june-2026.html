HUGE win on Aramingo AND 3rd Street!

June 2026 Newsletter

 

Huge Win: Concrete Protection for Aramingo!

At a packed community meeting in Olde Richmond, PennDOT announced it would install "Toronto-style" concrete barriers on both sides of Aramingo Ave bike lane between Lehigh and Sommerset where Glenn Colville was killed by a driver in April. 

Our advocacy through State Rep Hohenstein and Senator Nikil Saval and directly with PennDOT (you sent over 200 emails) helped push through significant internal resistance at PennDOT to make this possible! (KYW News Radio)

Getting protection for only this small portion falls short of our goals of replacing all flex-posts on Aramingo with concrete, but this is a big victory. Not only will this dangerous block be safer, this is the first time a Philly street will get Toronto style barriers, which are used in many other cities to make high-speed roads safer.

 

Another win: 3rd St Bike Lane finally installed! 

In early 2024, Philly Bike Action started pushing for a 3rd St bike lane. We ran a petition. Attended community meetings (CBS). Dozens of you spoke in support at City Hall and at loading zone regulation hearings. This week, the flex-post separated bike lane has FINALLY been installed from South to Market!

Join us in thanking Councilmember Mark Squilla for passing the legislation that made this bike lane a reality! Click below to send a pre-filled email.

Community Meeting: 48th & Woodland Playground 

The City of Philadelphia is working on a project to improve traffic safety around the 48th & Woodland Playground and the west side of the Grays Ferry Bridge. 

Tuesday, June 2nd (tomorrow!) 6:00PM - 8:00PM
Kingsessing Rec Center, 4901 Kingsessing Ave

Some of the best bicycle routes in Philadelphia converge towards this area, but they are not connected for these few blocks of hazardous high-speed car and truck traffic.

If you live or work in this area, please attend this public meeting to let the City know that bicyclists depend on safe and connected routes to travel between Southwest, West, and South Philadelphia. Your voice at this meeting will make a difference.
