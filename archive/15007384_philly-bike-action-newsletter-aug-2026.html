Remembering Dr. Barbara Friedes

In July, we held an extremely moving memorial for Dr. Barbara Friedes. Over 150 of you attended to honor her memory. Council President Kenyatta Johnson publicly committed his support for concrete protection for Spruce and Pine during his speech - a powerful moment after two years of advocacy.

We installed memorial bike lane protection in honor of Barbara. Painted with sunflowers (Barbara's favorite) to represent the 12 cyclists killed in Philly since Barbara was killed. It was filled collectively with water by everyone in attendance, a symbol of how we keep each other safe through our collective advocacy. Unfortunately, the memorial was removed by an unknown actor within hours of its installation.

Media Coverage: Inquirer (giftlink), KYW, NBC10, The Philthydelphian (reel)

West Market St Press Rally

In response to the pedestrian deaths of Meaza and Rosa last November, we held a press rally highlighting the need for urgent safety improvements to West Market St. Read coverage of the event in The Inquirer and KYW. 

This eight-block corridor between the Schuylkill River and 38th Street is one of the most dangerous high-priority corridors in Philadelphia. Short term, we are asking PennDOT to install speed cushions, reduce the speed limit from 30 mph to 25 mph, and add flex posts added to the existing bike lanes.

We thank State Representative Rick Krajewski and Councilmember Jamie Gauthier for their support in elevating this issue to PennDOT, the agency responsible.
