Two cyclists seriously injured in two weeks! Take Action!

Cyclist hit on Race St from Delaware River Trail

On Sept 20, Robert "Bawbee" Craskey was sent to the ICU after being hit by a car while crossing the I-95 on-ramp on Race St between 2nd St and Columbus Blvd. 

Crossing this on-ramp is the official (but unsafe) bike route for people connecting from the Delaware River Trail. Bawbee, bike shop owner and long-time supporter of Philly Bike Action, is recovering from serious injuries.

A year and a half ago, PBA raised concerns about this crossing with the City, but no action was taken. These crashes are preventable.

Send an email to Councilmember Squilla asking him to introduce legislation for a protected two-way bike lane on Race St for a safe trail connection into Center City.

Cyclist hit by SEPTA bus on South St Bridge

On Sept 9, a SEPTA bus struck a woman while she road in the bike lane on South St Bridge. She is stable, but she is seriously injured.

Almost 500 bikers use this bridge per hour during peak times. Yet, it has an unprotected bike lane that moves to the middle of the road (called a "mixing zone"). Mixing zones are supposed to reduce right hooks, but they are an inherently dangerous conflict points between bicycles and vehicles.

The City released a statement saying they are "actively designing safety improvements" to the east side of the bridge, but there is no timeline for implementation. 

Send an email asking Council President Johnson and the Streets Department to fast track these improvements before another person is hurt:

  • Add a bike signal, remove the mixing zone, and keep bike lane along curb
  • Add barriers to protect the bike lane
